### Stage 3: The 2-Step Quick-Win Pitch (Zero Rip-and-Replace)

* **The Problem:** ~300 quarterly expired cards trap ~$30k in cash and force staff to print paper notices or make phone calls.
* **The Fix:** SWS provides a CSV of 50 delinquent accounts. We send automated 1-click text alerts.
* **The Resident Experience:** Tap link $\rightarrow$ Apple Pay with FaceID $\rightarrow$ Paid in 5 seconds.
* **The Offer:** Risk-free pilot. Cash hits their merchant account; zero IT changes on their website.

#### Step 2: "Express Pay" by Phone Number on `suburbanwastemn.com`
* **The Problem:** 80% of login complaints come from residents who just want to pay a $94.50 bill, not manage an account.
* **The Fix:** Add a secondary button on their website: *"Express Pay (No Password Needed)"*.
* **The Flow:** Type 10-digit cell phone number $\rightarrow$ view balance $\rightarrow$ 1-tap Apple Pay.
* **The Win:** Kills login complaint calls overnight without touching Navusoft.
