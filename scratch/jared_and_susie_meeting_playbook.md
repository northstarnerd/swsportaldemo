### Stage 1: The Warm Icebreaker (First 3 Minutes)
* Shake hands, thank her for the time, and re-anchor on being an active local resident:
* > *"Susie, great to meet you in person! I really appreciate you taking time out of a busy week. First off, thank you to you and your drivers—our pickup on Promontory Drive is like clockwork every single week."*

---

### Stage 2: The Diagnostic (Listen 80% of the Time)
* Get her talking about the login crisis, customer calls, and the other vendor:
* > *"On the phone you mentioned customers have been struggling to log into the new portal and you’re working with another software company. Where are people getting stuck the most? What’s hitting your front desk's phones every day?"*
* **Listen for:**
  * Password reset loops and forgotten credentials.
  * Elderly residents confused by multi-step portal screens.
  * Office reps spending hours taking credit card numbers over the phone.
  * Frustration or delays with the other software company.

---

### Stage 3: The 2-Step Quick-Win Pitch (No Rip-and-Replace)

#### Step 1: The 30-Day Past-Due SMS Recovery Pilot
* > *"Let’s start with a zero-risk pilot on past-due accounts. Every quarter, cards expire and people forget to update them. You give me a simple export of 50 delinquent accounts. We send them an automated 1-click text. Residents tap once, pay via Apple Pay with FaceID in 5 seconds, and the cash hits your merchant account. If it doesn't collect cash and save your team phone calls, you owe nothing."*

#### Step 2: "Express Pay" by Phone Number on `suburbanwastemn.com`
* > *"To kill the login complaints without touching Navusoft, we add an 'Express Mobile Pay' button to your website. Homeowners just type their 10-digit cell phone number, see their $94.50 balance, and double-click Apple Pay or Google Pay. 80% of your login calls disappear because they don't even need an account or password to pay."*

---

### Stage 4: Planting the Advisory Seed (The Faris Consulting Bridge)
* If Susie brings up vendor headaches, delayed milestones, or technical confusion:
* > *"Susie, in addition to building these customer tools, my core background for 18 years is software architecture and technical advisory—often partnering with **Jared Faris at Faris Consulting**.*
* > *A lot of what we do is **Vendor Oversight** for business owners. Since you already have another company working on your portal, you don't need to fire them. If you ever feel like you need an experienced, independent technical set of eyes sitting on **your side of the table** to review what they're building and translate the tech jargon, we do that all the time. I'd be glad to set up a quick Zoom with Jared if that's ever helpful."*
