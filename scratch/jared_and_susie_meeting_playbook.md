# Part 1: Jared Faris Call Notes & Advisory Models

### 1. Jared's Core SMB Advisory Positioning Framework
* **The Situation:**
  * SWS is growing, established (~19 years), and doing really well.
  * But they’ve reached a point where technology (Navusoft ERP, mobile portals, payments) is **both super expensive and mission-critical** to their business.
  * Nobody inside SWS is a full-time technology leader—and why would they be? Their expertise is running trucks, route logistics, container management, and taking care of customers.
  * It doesn’t make sense for a 50-truck hauler to hire a full-time \$250k+ CIO or CTO.
* **The Solution (What We Do):**
  * We come in and **plug that gap** on a flexible advisory basis.
  * We help the owners and leadership team think about where tech fits into their strategy.
  * We make sure they have the right providers and the right services.
  * **We help them hold those providers accountable—and call bullshit on them if they aren’t delivering.**
* **The Bottom-Line Message:**
  * We come in as guides to help them make the right decisions and let them get back to the journey they are on **without having to become a tech company**.

### 2. The Core Metaphor: The "General Contractor" (GC)
* SWS understands blue-collar trades, trucks, and facilities.
* The other waste software company is a **subcontractor**.
* Without a General Contractor representing the owner, subs overcharge, make excuses, and do sloppy work.
* Patrick + Jared act as the **General Contractor for Technology**: inspecting work, sitting on vendor calls, calling bullshit on technical jargon, and protecting SWS's bottom line.

### 3. Commercial Baseline & The 3 Engagement Models
* **Value Floor:** Not worth doing anything that isn't at least **$1,000 – $2,000 / month**.
* **Model 1: "I'll teach / advise you" (~$1.5k/mo):** Monthly review of vendor proposals; sounding board for Susie/Paul.
* **Model 2: "I'll ride along with you" (~$3k/mo — Target Sweet Spot):** Active vendor oversight; join bi-weekly vendor calls; review technical specs; keep vendor accountable (split between Patrick & Jared).
* **Model 3: "I'll take the wheel and run this for you" (~$5k+/mo):** Fractional CTO; full technical ownership; driving the roadmap.

* **The Flow:** Type 10-digit cell phone number $\rightarrow$ view balance $\rightarrow$ 1-tap Apple Pay.
* **The Win:** Kills login complaint calls overnight without touching Navusoft.
