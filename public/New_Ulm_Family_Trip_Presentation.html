Leg 1 • Road Trip Start ⏰ 9:30 AM Departure

Eden Prairie ➔ St. Peter

📍 Route: US-169 South (~55 mins, 48 miles)

☀️

Smooth Morning Departure

Hitting the road by 9:30 AM lets the toddler stay in high spirits and catches the quietest stretch of highway before lunchtime traffic.

🌳

Minnesota River Valley Views

The drive along US-169 follows the sweeping curve of the Minnesota River Valley, passing Shakopee, Jordan, and Belle Plaine with lush green bluffs.

💡

Toddler Road Trip Tip: Keep a spill-proof snack cup, travel sippy cup, and a small bin of car toys within arm's reach from the front passenger seat.

🎪 Fresh Baked Goods & Shaded Play
Historic Bandstand & mature shade trees at Minnesota Square Park Click to Zoom
Stop 1 • Leg-Stretch & Snacks ⏰ 10:25 AM – 10:55 AM (30 mins)

Minnesota Square Park

📍 South Minnesota Ave, St. Peter, MN

🥐

Local Saturday Farmers Market

Runs Saturday mornings until 12:00 PM. Grab fresh local pastries, fresh-picked berries for the kids, and hot artisan coffee for parents.

🛝

Shaded Playground & Clean Restrooms

Full playground structure nestled under enormous mature oak trees with accessible clean public restrooms right next to the path.

🅿️

Pro Parking Tip: Turn onto South 3rd Street or Grace Street for quiet, shaded curb parking directly alongside the grass and play structure.

🔔 Schonlau Park & Downtown Cafes
Schonlau Park on Minnesota Street, home of the Glockenspiel Click to Zoom
Stop 2 • Downtown New Ulm ⏰ 11:40 AM – 1:00 PM (80 mins)

The Glockenspiel & Lunch

📍 Schonlau Park (4th N & Minnesota St), New Ulm

🎶

The 12:00 PM Noon Chime Performance

Arrive at Schonlau Park by 11:45 AM. At precisely 12:00 PM, 37 bells chime musical tunes while 12 handcrafted animated figures rotate on stage. Kids are mesmerized!

🍽️

Minnesota Street Walk & Lunch

Walk directly along Minnesota Street for family-friendly casual bakeries, German delis, fresh soft pretzels, and outdoor shaded bistro tables.

🔔

Sound preview available: Click the "Glockenspiel Chime" button in the top bar to hear the musical carillon chime!

🌿 Shaded Riverside Stroller Trails
Gentle Cottonwood River flowing through Flandrau State Park Click to Zoom
Stop 3 • Nature & Serenity ⏰ 1:15 PM – 2:30 PM (75 mins)

Flandrau State Park

📍 1300 Summit Ave, New Ulm (5 mins south of downtown)

💤

The Toddler Stroller Nap Sanctuary

Wide, flat, heavily shaded walking trails alongside the Cottonwood River. The gentle river breeze and rustling cottonwood leaves create the ultimate natural white noise for naptime.

🦅

Scenic Nature & Wildlife

Songbirds, turtles sunbathing on logs, clear water shallows, and towering sandstone cliffs. Perfect peaceful recharge for parents while baby sleeps soundly.

👟

Trail Tip: Park near the main Cottonwood River Trailhead. Trails are packed dirt and crushed limestone—very smooth and easy for standard strollers.

🦌 Peacocks, Deer Park & Estate Gardens
Live deer in the on-site Schell Deer Park enclosure Click to Zoom
Stop 4 • Wildlife, Nature & Heritage ⏰ 2:45 PM – 3:45 PM (60 mins)

August Schell Grounds

📍 1860 Schell Rd, New Ulm (Secluded Cottonwood River Valley)

🦚

Free-Roaming Peacocks & On-Site Deer Park

A flock of colorful resident peacocks roams freely through the flowerbeds and lawns, posing for photos. Behind the mansion, visit the historic 1880s deer park enclosure where kids can watch gentle white-tailed deer up close.

🌺

German Botanical Gardens & Scenic Valley

Nestled down in a private, heavily wooded river valley. Stroll along paved paths surrounded by lush flowers, stone retaining walls, fountains, and towering black walnut trees.

🥤

1919 Draft Root Beer & Shaded Biergarten

Fresh draft 1919 Root Beer on tap and giant warm pretzels for the kids, plus authentic German craft lagers for parents on the shaded garden patio.

🌸

Family Friendly: Paved, flat stroller paths throughout the estate. Walking the outdoor botanical gardens and visiting the deer park is completely free!

⛰️ 102-Ft Monument & Valley Panorama
The 102-foot Hermann the German monument standing high atop the bluff Click to Zoom
Stop 5 • Bluff Lookout & Play ⏰ 3:50 PM – 4:30 PM (40 mins)

Hermann Heights Park

📍 10 Monument St, New Ulm, MN

🦅

Minnesota River Valley Panoramic Overlook

Standing at the high bluff overlook offers 360-degree views of the church spires, river curves, and forested hills of Brown County.

🏃‍♂️

Modern Park Playground

Features an expansive playground structure with slides, swings, and climbing sets. Ideal for burning off any remaining toddler wiggles before the car ride home.

📸

Photo Spot: Great family portrait location right in front of the stone rotunda with the afternoon sun illuminating the statue above.

⚓ Riverside Park & Nature Center
Historic Riverside Park and grounds along the Minnesota River in New Ulm Click to Zoom
Backup / Bonus Stop ⏰ 1:00 PM – 4:00 PM (Flexible)

Riverside Park

📍 101 S Front St, New Ulm, MN

⚓

Pirate Ship Adventure & Play

A hit with young kids! A large wooden pirate ship play structure equipped with ship wheels, lookouts, and slides directly on the riverfront.

🐢

Riverside History & Nature Learning Center

Open 1:00 PM – 4:00 PM on select weekends with interactive river wildlife displays, taxidermy, and hands-on nature discovery for little learners.

🔀

When to use this stop: If the nap finishes early or you want an extra 20 minutes of play before leaving town.

Final Leg • Heading Home ⏰ 4:30 PM – 5:00 PM Departure

New Ulm ➔ Eden Prairie

📍 Route: US-14 E to US-169 N (~85 mins, 78 miles)

✨

A Full Day of Happy Memories

Chiming Glockenspiel bells, shaded forest river trails, live peacocks and deer, farmers market sweets, and playground laughter—all within a calm single-day outing.

🍔

Dinner Along the Way (Optional)

If anyone gets hungry, Belle Plaine or Jordan make convenient 5-minute pull-offs for a quick casual dinner before bedtime.

🛏️

Bedtime Timing: Arriving home around 6:00 PM – 6:15 PM leaves plenty of time for bath, dinner, and a peaceful on-time bedtime.
